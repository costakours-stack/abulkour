Install Abulkour on Android

Works anywhere with internet. Your computer is not needed.

https://abulkour-api.onrender.com/download
Steps
  1. Scan the code with your phone camera (or type the address into Chrome).
  2. Tap Download the app, open the file, allow installs from Chrome, tap Install.
  3. If you had the old MarketPulse app, uninstall it first.
  4. Open Abulkour: Alerts, Server address, paste your access code, tap Save & test.
